name: prepare-platform-export
description: Compose reusable video export presets for platform-oriented output.
---

Use `formats_list` and `formats_resolve`; presets are configuration, not separate tools. Platform labels are advisory and do not certify current upload limits. Compose canvas, codec and quality fragments; resolve conflicts using explicit user choices.

Choose the platform surface and crop/pad behavior where ambiguous. Geometry changes require geometry scope, and explicit audio transcoding/drop requires format scope. Preparing an export never authorizes social publishing, captions, music or Canva upload. File-size targeting is unavailable. Verify actual dimensions/codec and disclose limits.
