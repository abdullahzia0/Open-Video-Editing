---
name: add-text-and-cta
description: Add titles, lower-third text, hooks or CTA overlays with Open Video Editing.
---

Use a `title` operation with supplied or authorized drafted text, start/end times, style and optional fade. All times refer to the final edited timeline. A CTA overlay near the end differs from an appended end card; the latter is not implemented.

Do not invent claims, names or endorsements. Current title templates render near the lower part of the frame. Custom positioning and arbitrary graphics are unavailable. Declare text scope only when that is the requested change; verify the render before claiming placement.
