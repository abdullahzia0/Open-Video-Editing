description: Apply conservative lighting, color, noise or sharpening edits without changing unrelated video properties.
---

Match the specific requested effect to `lighting`, `color`, `denoise` or `sharpen`; declare only those allowed effects. Lighting alone does not authorize sharpening, denoising, cropping or text. Visual denoising is distinct from audio noise reduction, which is not implemented.

Read capability availability and plan warnings. Conventional resizing can upscale dimensions; do not call it neural detail restoration. Stabilization, HDR processing, learned low-light restoration and neural enhancement are unavailable in this release. Return actual validation evidence and recommend visual review for aesthetic quality.
