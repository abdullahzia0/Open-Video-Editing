name: canva-handoff
description: Check Open Video Editing design-provider readiness before a Canva handoff.
---

Read `connections_status`. The bundled Canva adapter is unavailable and `designs_execute` returns an actionable error. No credentials alone will enable it. Do not invent authentication, upload or editing success.

If the user needs a handoff now, produce the requested local asset with the supported engine and explain manual upload in Canva. Do not upload anywhere without existing authorization. Future adapters must distinguish asset upload, insertion into a design, committed edits and exported files; one receipt is not evidence of the others.
